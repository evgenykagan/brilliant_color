How the recommendation works

Choose an order quantity: 100, 200, or 300 cases. Each choice branches into three possible demand outcomes.

Scenario profit
min(order, demand) × selling price − order × cost − max(order − demand, 0) × salvage cost

Expected profit
Sum of each scenario’s profit × its probability

Probabilities are your weights divided by their total. The recommendation selects the order with the largest expected profit. Tied options are all shown.

With the starting assumptions, expected profits are $7,500, $11,333.33, and $11,500 for orders of 100, 200, and 300 cases.
