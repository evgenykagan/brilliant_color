BC-6 / WEEKLY ORDER PLANNER

One decision. Three possible futures.

Follow each order choice through the possible demand outcomes. The best choice maximizes expected weekly profit, including a salvage cost for every unsold case.

Demand weights are normalized to probabilities. Equal weights give each outcome a ⅓ chance. Salvage is treated as an additional cost, charged only on unsold cases.

□ Decision: choose an order○ Chance: demand occursEnd of branch: net profit
How it works

Sold cases = min(order, demand). Unsold cases = max(order − demand, 0).

Profit = sold cases × selling price − order × purchase cost − unsold cases × salvage cost.

Expected profit is the sum of each branch’s profit multiplied by its demand probability. Compare the expected profits at each order node; the highest is highlighted. Demand above the order is lost, and unsold cases incur the salvage cost without generating revenue.
